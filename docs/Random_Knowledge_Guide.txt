RANDOM KNOWLEDGE GUIDE
======================

1. What is photosynthesis?
Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to make sugars. Oxygen is released as a by-product.

2. What is the solar system?
The solar system consists of the Sun and the objects held by its gravity, including eight planets, dwarf planets, moons, asteroids, and comets.

3. What is EPF?
Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) is a retirement savings scheme for eligible employees in India. Contributions are generally made by both the employee and employer under applicable rules.

4. Why do we recycle?
Recycling processes used materials into new products. It can reduce waste sent to landfills and lower demand for some raw materials.

5. What is an API?
An Application Programming Interface (API) defines how software components communicate and exchange data.

6. What is Python?
Python is a general-purpose programming language often used for automation, web development, data analysis, and artificial intelligence.

7. How can people save electricity?
Common methods include switching off unused lights, using efficient appliances, improving insulation, and choosing LED lighting.

8. What is a healthy study habit?
A useful study routine includes clear goals, focused sessions, short breaks, practice questions, and regular revision.

9. What causes rain?
Rain forms when water vapor cools and condenses into droplets in clouds. When droplets become heavy enough, they fall to the ground.

10. What is cybersecurity?
Cybersecurity involves protecting devices, networks, applications, and information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disruption.

11. What is a database?
A database is an organized collection of information that can be stored, searched, updated, and managed.

12. What is teamwork?
Teamwork is people coordinating their skills and efforts to achieve a shared goal.
